# Critique rubric

Score each slide 0–3 on five criteria. Max 15 per slide. Show the table, then the top 5 fixes.

## Criteria

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Clarity** | Cannot tell what the slide says | Point is buried in text | Point is clear after reading | Point is clear in 3 seconds |
| **Evidence** | Claims with no support, or invented-looking numbers | Numbers without source, period, or definition | Sourced, but weak for the stage | Strongest evidence the stage allows, sourced and defined |
| **Headline** | Missing or a label ("Team") | Topic phrase ("Our market opportunity") | Claim, but vague | Full-sentence takeaway a partner could repeat |
| **Visual** | Clip art, wall of text, or decorative only | Visual present but unrelated to the point | Supports the point | Makes the point faster than text would |
| **Position** | Slide hurts the story here or is redundant | Belongs in appendix | Fine where it is | Placed to lead with strength or answer the next question |

## Score bands

| Slide total | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 13–15 | Strong | Leave it |
| 9–12 | Works, has a gap | Fix the lowest criterion |
| 5–8 | Weak | Rewrite headline and evidence |
| 0–4 | Hurts the deck | Cut, merge, or rebuild |

Overall deck: average slide score plus the headline test (pass/fail) and the 30-second skim test (what a partner retains).

## Ranking the top 5 fixes

Rank by impact on the investor decision, in this order of weight:

1. Anything that breaks trust: unsourced or inconsistent numbers, inflated traction labels, "no competitors", top-down-only market.
2. Missing core answer: no why-now, no clear customer, no ask/milestone, or any clarity-check question (what you do, progress, why you, what you understand that competitors don't, how you make money) the deck alone cannot answer.
3. Story order: strongest asset buried after slide 6.
4. Headlines that are labels.
5. Visual and density problems.

A design fix never outranks a trust or story fix.

## Investor-objection checklist by stage

Use with `## Biggest risk` from company.md. For each, find the slide that answers it.

**Idea / validation**
- Why are you the team for this?
- How do you know the pain is real and frequent?
- Will anyone pay, and how much?
- Why has no one done this already?
- What will you prove with this money?

**MVP**
- Are users coming back without you pushing them?
- Who pays, and is the buyer the user?
- How do you get the next 50 customers?
- What stops an incumbent from copying this?
- Is this a feature or a company?

**Early traction / growth**
- Is growth from one channel or one customer?
- What is retention by cohort?
- What does it cost to acquire a customer, and how long to pay back?
- Why does growth continue at 3x the current size?
- What milestone does this round reach, and is it enough for the next round?

## Red flags to call out explicitly
- Cumulative charts without monthly view.
- Pilots, LOIs, or waitlists labelled as customers.
- Market slide with only a top-down figure.
- Advisors given more space than founders.
- Valuation or terms on slides at pre-seed.
- Use of funds without a milestone.
- More than 14 main slides.
