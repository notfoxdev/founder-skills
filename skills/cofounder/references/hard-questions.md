# Hard questions bank

Use in Sounding board mode. Filter by the founder's stage first, then weight toward the theme of the stated biggest risk. Ask one per turn. The "weak answer" column tells you when to ask a follow-up instead of moving on.

Themes: customer, market, product, team, money, founder.

## How to use

1. Pick 3–5 candidates from the founder's stage. Include at least one from the biggest-risk theme and one `founder` question per session.
2. Skip any question the journal already answers with evidence.
3. If the answer matches the weak-answer pattern, ask one follow-up: "What happened the last time?", "Who, specifically?", "How do you know?", or "What would you do if that were false?"
4. A question the founder cannot answer well is the output of the session. Log it as an `[ASSUMPTION]` in the journal.
5. Questions from one stage earlier are fair game if the founder skipped that stage's work.

## Idea

| # | Theme | Question | Weak answer sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | customer | Name three people who have this problem. When did each last run into it? | Categories ("SMBs", "busy parents") instead of names |
| 2 | customer | What do they do about it today, and what does that cost them in money or hours? | "Nothing, there's no solution" |
| 3 | market | Why hasn't this been solved already? What changed recently that makes it solvable now? | "No one has thought of it" |
| 4 | product | What is the smallest thing you could deliver by hand next week to see if anyone wants it? | Describes an app with accounts and a dashboard |
| 5 | founder | Why are you the person to build this, rather than someone with ten years in the industry? | Passion alone, no access or insight |
| 6 | founder | If this idea were dead in three months, what would you work on instead? | No answer, or an identical idea |
| 7 | money | Who pays: the user, their manager, or someone else? Have you asked any of them? | Assumes user equals buyer |
| 8 | market | Where do these customers already gather, online or offline, where you could reach 50 of them this month? | "Social media", "ads" |
| 9 | customer | Is this a top-three problem for them, or something they'd fix if it were free? | "It's a pain for everyone" |
| 10 | team | What part of this can't you do yourself, and how will you cover it for the next 90 days? | "I'll hire" with no budget |
