# Metrics by stage

Pick one North Star and 3–5 inputs. All ranges below are rough heuristics for orientation, drawn from widely discussed practitioner rules of thumb. They vary by segment, price point, and geography. They are not targets and not benchmarks to report to investors. When quoting one, say "rough range, not a target" and prefer the founder's own trend over any external number.

## Choosing rules

- **North Star:** measures value customers receive, weekly, movable within weeks, leads to revenue. One only.
- **Inputs:** things the founder controls this week. Mix activity (calls, experiments shipped) and conversion (rate between two steps). At least one input tests the biggest risk.
- **Stage first, model second.** At idea and validation, the metric is learning speed, not growth.
- **Avoid:** cumulative totals, page views, downloads, registered users without activity, anything with no decision attached.

## Idea

Goal: find a painful, frequent problem with a reachable buyer.

| Model | North Star | Inputs |
|---|---|---|
| Any | Problem interviews per week that confirm the pain pattern | Interviews booked; interviews held; % confirming the specific pain; # who describe a current workaround or spend; follow-up requests |

Rough ranges: many founders aim for ~10–20 problem interviews per week while searching; a pattern usually shows after ~15–30 conversations within one segment. If fewer than roughly a third confirm the pain after ~20 interviews, revisit the segment or problem.

## Validation

Goal: prove someone will commit (money, time, data) before full build.

| Model | North Star | Inputs |
|---|---|---|
| B2B SaaS | Committed design partners or paid pilots | Qualified conversations; demo/prototype sessions; % asking for next step; LOIs or pilot proposals sent; pilots signed |
| B2C | Pre-orders or waitlist sign-ups from a stated, targeted source | Landing page visitors by channel; visitor-to-signup %; signup-to-deposit %; smoke-test cost per signup |
| Marketplace | Matches completed manually (concierge) in one niche | Supply onboarded; demand requests; % requests matched; repeat requests |

Rough ranges: a targeted landing page converting visitors to sign-ups in the low single digits to ~10%+ is commonly discussed; deposits or pre-orders are far stronger evidence than sign-ups at any rate.

## MVP

Goal: users get value repeatedly without the founder pushing.

| Model | North Star | Inputs |
|---|---|---|
| B2B SaaS | Weekly active accounts doing the core action | Activation rate (signup → core action); time to first value; accounts active in week 4 of their cohort; trial → paid %; support requests per active account |
| B2C | Weekly active users doing the core action | Day-1 / day-7 / day-30 retention; activation rate; core actions per active user; referral or share rate |
| Marketplace | Weekly successful transactions in the first market | Liquidity (% listings or requests that transact in a set window); repeat rate both sides; time to match |

Rough ranges: for consumer apps, a cohort retention curve that flattens rather than trending to zero matters more than the level; for B2B, weekly usage by most design partners after onboarding is a common bar. Trial-to-paid in B2B SaaS varies widely by motion (low single digits for free sign-ups, much higher for sales-assisted trials).

## Early traction

Goal: repeatable acquisition and revenue growth with retention.

| Model | North Star | Inputs |
|---|---|---|
| B2B SaaS | Net new MRR per week (or MRR) | Qualified pipeline created; win rate; sales cycle length; logo churn; expansion MRR; activation of new accounts |
| B2C | Weekly paying users or weekly revenue | New users by channel; CAC by channel; conversion to paid; month-2 retention; ARPU |
| Marketplace | Weekly GMV in active markets | Supply and demand growth; liquidity; take rate; repeat rate; CAC per side |

Rough ranges: early-stage B2B SaaS growth of roughly 10–20% month over month is often discussed as strong at small revenue; monthly logo churn for SMB SaaS is often cited in the low single digits, lower for mid-market and enterprise. Treat these as context only.

## Growth

Goal: efficient, scalable growth.

| Model | North Star | Inputs |
|---|---|---|
| B2B SaaS | ARR (or net new ARR) | Net revenue retention; CAC payback months; pipeline coverage; ramped rep productivity; gross margin |
| B2C | Weekly or monthly active paying users | Cohort LTV; CAC payback by channel; paid vs organic mix; retention by cohort |
| Marketplace | GMV or net revenue | Market-level liquidity; take rate; contribution margin per transaction; cross-side network effects (repeat and referral) |

Rough ranges: CAC payback under roughly 12–18 months and net revenue retention above ~100% are common reference points for B2B SaaS; consumer and marketplace norms differ widely by category.

## Fundraising period (any stage)

Add to the scoreboard temporarily, not as the North Star:

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Intros requested | This week |
| First meetings held | This week |
| % to second meeting | Second meetings ÷ first meetings, rolling |
| Active diligence | Count |
| Soft-circled vs target | Amount and % |

## Input metric quality check

For each candidate input, answer yes to all four or replace it:

1. Can the founder change it this week?
2. Is there a plausible causal link to the North Star?
3. Is it measured the same way every week?
4. Would a change in it lead to a different decision?
