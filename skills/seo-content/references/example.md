## Model answer

# Content plan — Ledgerline — 2026-10-23

**Start this week:** "How to see which clients are blocked at month-end close" — it's the problem your interviews summary leads with, and you lived it for four years.
**Time cap:** 4 hours a week, proposed. One page every week or two. | **Expectation:** search won't bring the 3 paying firms in your 90-day goal. Those come from the 2 pilots and the 9 in-band waitlist firms. Content is a side bet, so I've picked pages you can also send to those firms this month.
**Demand data:** no tool this session. Every volume is `[NEED]`; pages are ranked on customer evidence and your experience.

On the 30 AI posts: the case for it is real, since more pages give you more chances to show up. The problem is that on a brand-new domain, 30 thin posts teach search engines that the site is thin, and a partner who lands on one will spot it in a paragraph. Your edge is four years running close at a 9-person firm, and a generator doesn't have that. Test it instead: publish the six below by early December, check impressions at weeks 8–12, and only add pages where the first ones show life. Your call. I've logged the six-page plan as proposed; if you'd rather run the 30, say so and I'll log that with the same week-12 check.

**Not now:**

- 30 generated posts: see above.
- Programmatic pages ("close checklist for <client industry>", times 40): not until each page carries data you own that's different per page, at least 5 hand-written pages earn impressions, and the pattern has measured demand.
