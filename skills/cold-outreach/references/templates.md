# Outreach templates by use case

Read this when drafting. Templates are skeletons, not copy to paste. Replace every `{{field}}` with verified, specific content; if a field cannot be filled truthfully, rewrite the sentence or drop it. Bodies stay under 90 words, follow-ups under 60.

Standard fields:
- `{{first_name}}`, `{{company}}`, `{{role}}`
- `{{reason_for_you}}`: the specific, verified reason this person, now (a post, a hire, a launch, a talk, a mutual connection who agreed to be named)
- `{{problem_in_their_words}}`: the problem phrased the way their segment describes it
- `{{credibility}}`: one founder-provided fact (background, relevant result, who else you have spoken to, without naming people who did not agree)
- `{{ask}}`: one small request
- `{{sender}}`, `{{sender_title}}`, `{{company_one_liner}}`

Opt-out line (vary wording, keep it plain): "If this isn't relevant, just say so and I won't follow up."
Add a postal address in the signature for commercial emails to US recipients.

## 1. Customer discovery

Goal: a 15–20 minute conversation to learn, not to sell.

**Touch 1**
Subject: `{{topic}} at {{company}}` / `question about {{workflow}}` / `{{role}} workflow`

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> {{reason_for_you}}. I'm researching how {{segment}} handle {{problem_in_their_words}}, before we build anything further.
>
> I'm not selling anything. I'd value 15 minutes to hear how you do it today and what's frustrating about it. Happy to share what I'm learning across the other conversations.
>
> Would a short call next week work?
>
> {{sender}}

**Touch 2 (day 3–4)**
> One more detail in case it's useful: so far {{founder-provided pattern, e.g. "most teams I've spoken to do this in a spreadsheet"}}. I'm curious whether that matches your experience. Even a one-line reply helps.

**Touch 3 (day 7–8, smaller ask)**
> If a call is too much, one question instead: how do you currently handle {{specific step}}?

**Breakup (day 13–14)**
> I'll stop here. If {{problem}} becomes a priority later, I'd still like to hear how you approach it. Thanks, {{first_name}}.

## 2. Sales

Goal: a short call or a reply that qualifies interest. Lead with their problem, not your features.

**Touch 1**
Subject: `{{trigger}}` / `{{outcome}} for {{company}}` / `{{peer_segment}} and {{problem}}`

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> {{reason_for_you, ideally a trigger: new hire, launch, expansion, regulation}}. Teams in that position often run into {{problem_in_their_words}}.
>
> We built {{company_one_liner}}. {{credibility: founder-provided result or customer, only with permission}}.
>
> Worth a 20-minute look to see if it fits {{company}}? If timing's wrong, say so and I'll leave it.
>
> {{sender}}

**Touch 2 (day 3–4, new value)**
> A concrete example: {{how a similar customer used it, founder-provided, anonymised unless permitted}}. Happy to show you the same thing on {{company}}'s setup.

**Touch 3 (day 7–8, yes/no)**
> Is {{problem}} something you're working on this quarter, or not a priority right now? Either answer helps.

**Breakup**
> Closing the loop. If {{problem}} comes up later, my inbox is open.

## 3. Partnerships

Goal: an exploratory call about a specific, mutual opportunity.

**Touch 1**
Subject: `{{their_product}} + {{your_product}}` / `{{shared_customer_segment}}` / `integration idea`

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> {{reason_for_you: their role in partnerships, a public partner programme, an overlapping customer base}}. We both serve {{shared_segment}}: you handle {{their_job}}, we handle {{your_job}}.
>
> {{specific idea: an integration, co-marketing piece, referral arrangement}}, which would give your customers {{benefit to them}}.
>
> Open to a 20-minute call to see if it's worth exploring?
>
> {{sender}}

**Touch 2**
> To make it concrete: {{one-paragraph sketch, or what you would build or contribute first}}. We'd do the heavy lifting on our side.

**Breakup**
> I'll leave it here. If partnerships around {{shared_segment}} become a focus, I'd be glad to pick this up.

## 4. Hiring

Goal: a casual conversation, not an application. Respect that they likely have a job.

**Touch 1**
Subject: `your work on {{project}}` / `{{role}} at {{company}}` / `{{specific skill}}`

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> {{reason_for_you: specific work, repo, talk, or post, verified}}. That's close to the problem we're working on: {{problem, one line}}.
>
> I'm {{sender_title}} at {{company}}, {{stage and one fact: funded by / team size / traction, founder-provided}}. We're hiring a {{role}} to {{scope of the work}}.
>
> Open to a 20-minute chat, no application needed? If you're not looking, no reply needed.
>
> {{sender}}

**Touch 2**
> One more detail: {{what makes the role distinct: ownership, technical challenge, compensation range if the founder is willing to share}}.

**Breakup**
> I'll stop here. If timing changes, the door's open.

Rule: max 3 touches for hiring. Never contact someone through their current employer's systems.

## 5. Investor intros

Warm intros beat cold for most investors. Pipeline, targeting, and round timing belong to the fundraising skill.

### 5a. Ask a connector for an intro
Subject: `intro to {{investor_name}}?`

> Hi {{connector_first_name}},
>
> We're raising {{round, founder-provided}} for {{company_one_liner}}. {{investor_name}} at {{firm}} looks like a strong fit because {{specific reason: thesis, past investment, public statement, verified}}.
>
> Would you be comfortable introducing us? I've added a short forwardable note below. No problem at all if not.
>
> {{sender}}

### 5b. Forwardable blurb (connector forwards this)
> {{company}} {{one-liner}}. {{traction, founder-provided only, one line}}. {{team credibility, one line}}. Raising {{amount}} {{round}} to {{milestone}}. {{sender}} would value 20 minutes with {{investor_name}} because {{specific fit}}.

Keep under 80 words. No attachments; offer the deck on request.

### 5c. Direct cold to an investor (when no warm path exists)
Subject: `{{company}}: {{one specific traction or insight}}`

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> {{reason_for_you: their thesis, a portfolio company in an adjacent space, a post, verified}}. We're building {{company_one_liner}}.
>
> {{traction, founder-provided, one line}}. Raising {{amount}} {{round}}.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute call? Deck available if useful.
>
> {{sender}}

Max 2 follow-ups for investors, each with a real update (new customer, metric, milestone).

## LinkedIn connection note (under 200 characters)

> Hi {{first_name}}, {{reason_for_you, one clause}}. I'm {{working on / researching}} {{topic}} and would value your take. Open to connecting?

## X DM (keep under 280 characters by choice; the platform allows more)

> Hi {{first_name}}, your post on {{topic}} ({{what it said, briefly}}) matches what I'm seeing with {{segment}}. Building {{short one-liner}}. Open to a 15-min call sometime?

## Rewrite patterns

| Weak | Why it fails | Better |
|---|---|---|
| "Hope you're doing well" | Filler, takes the preview line | Start with the reason for you |
| "I'd love to pick your brain" | Vague, one-sided | "Could I ask you 3 questions about how you handle X?" |
| "We're the leading AI platform for..." | Unprovable, generic | One plain sentence on what it does |
| "Do you have 30 minutes this week?" | Big ask, high friction | "Would 15 minutes next week work?" |
| "Just bumping this to the top of your inbox" | Adds nothing | Add a new example, finding, or smaller ask |
| "I noticed you're the head of X at Y" | Not a reason, just a fact | Why that role matters for this message |
