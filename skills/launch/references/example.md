# Worked example: launch

> Illustrative and fictional (Ledgerline fixture). Calibrates depth and format only; never reuse its content.

**Request:** "Plan our launch for when the pilots convert."

**Context used:** `evals/fixture-company.md` as `.foundora/company.md`. 62 waitlist signups (9 from firms in the 3–15 staff band), 2 free 30-day concierge pilots start next week, no revenue, no paid budget, about 7 months of runway, 90-day goal of 3 firms paying at least $150/month. Buyer is the managing partner, B2B. Not in the file: founder's LinkedIn reach, time zone, billing setup. Hard parts shown: the gate fails Complete (concierge), so this is not a public launch; the date hangs on a milestone; Product Hunt, Show HN, and marketplaces are scored and skipped.

---

## Recommendation
Go smaller: a paid pilot cohort offered to your own waitlist, not a public launch. Trigger: at least 1 of the 2 pilots signs at $150/month or more by Friday 2026-12-04. Launch day is Tuesday 2026-12-15, after December close. Target: enough new paying firms to reach 3 in total by 2027-01-18 (the 90-day mark from 2026-10-20).
