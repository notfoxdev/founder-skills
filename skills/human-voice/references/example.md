## Context used

- company.md: 2 firms agreed to a free 30-day concierge pilot starting next week; no revenue; Priya is cofounder and CTO; US spelling; tone direct.
- No voice notes in the journal and no writing samples. Voice comes from the founder's message above: short sentences, contractions ("we've got", "I'd"), plain verbs ("trying it out"), feelings said straight. The lowercase is chat habit, not a writing habit, so the post uses normal capitals.
- Mode: Rewrite. Channel: LinkedIn post, 60–200 words, news in the first line.
- Fact ledger: (1) two firms, (2) "first two customers, officially signed", (3) starting next week, (4) "leading" firms, (5) they'll use Ledgerline for close, (6) see status as it happens, (7) stop rework, (8) "countless hours" saved monthly, (9) Priya is the cofounder, (10) call to action: bookkeeping firms should get in touch.
- Conflict: fact 2 says "signed customers"; company.md says a free pilot with no revenue. That's a fact problem, not a style one.
- No questions asked: the post goes up tomorrow, so open items sit in the text as markers.

## Facts check

Kept 9 of 10 facts. Added: none (the "nervous and excited" line is yours, from your message). Changed: "signed customers" became "free 30-day pilot", to match company.md. Dropped: "our amazing community of supporters", because it names nobody and says nothing a reader can check. Your call if you want it back with names. "Real-time" became "as it happens"; same claim, plainer words.

## Open items

- [NEED: "signed customers" vs company.md "free 30-day concierge pilot, no revenue"] I used the company.md version. If the firms have signed something or are paying, tell me and I'll propose a company.md update; the post can then say so.
- [NEED: source for "leading"] Nothing in company.md backs it. A partner at a competing firm will read it as puffery. I'd drop it, but that's your call.
- [NEED: what the pilot firms actually touch] If you two are doing the work by hand, "they'll use it to see which clients are blocked" overstates it. Honest version if so: "For a month, Priya and I will run their close checklist with them and see whether partners stop chasing status."
- [NEED: hours saved] The pilots haven't started, so there's no measured number. Unless you have one, cut that sentence and post it in December with the pilot result.
- Optional, not added: company.md says you ran ops at a 9-person bookkeeping firm for 4 years. One line on that would make the post more credible to the exact people you want to hear from. Say the word and I'll add it.
