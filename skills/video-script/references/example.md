# Worked example: video-script

> Illustrative and fictional (Ledgerline fixture). Calibrates depth and format only; never reuse its content.

**Request:** "Write a 60-second demo video script."

**Context used:** `evals/fixture-company.md` as `.foundora/company.md`. Validation stage, no revenue, 2 concierge pilots start next week, no product UI in the file. Buyer is the managing partner; the problem is "can't see which clients are blocked". Tone: direct, tables over paragraphs. Placement not stated, so `[ASSUMPTION]` landing page `how-it-works` plus the founder's LinkedIn. Hard parts shown: there is no product to demo (prototype label and a fallback), the word budget computed and met, a muted read test.

---

## Recommendation
A 60s demo on launch-video beats, opening on the messy close spreadsheet with "Which client is blocked?", then a clickable prototype of the close board labelled "Prototype" on screen for the whole product moment. The pilots run concierge, so there is no real product to film yet; the shoot is blocked on `[NEED: 3 prototype screens from Priya: board, client checklist, partner summary]`.

## Brief
- Format: product demo on launch-video beats (under 90s rule) | Length: 60s | Aspect: 16:9 master, 1:1 cut | Platform: landing page, LinkedIn `[ASSUMPTION]`
- Spine: After watching, a managing partner knows Ledgerline shows every client's close status, including who is blocked and why, and joins the waitlist.
- CTA: "Join the waitlist at ledgerline dot app" -> ledgerline.app
- Word budget: 115–135 (calc: 60s x 2.25 = 135 ceiling; 85% floor = 115; visual-only time is 5s of 60, under 30%) | Script total: 127

## Hook options
1. Pattern: the painful moment | Visual: sample close spreadsheet, five fill colors, cursor scrolling fast, lands on a cell comment "is this reconciled??" | First line: "Which client is blocked?" | Text: "Which client is blocked?" | Payoff at: 0:20 | Why: every partner has scrolled this sheet; it reads with sound off on LinkedIn.
2. Pattern: founder confession | Visual: founder to camera, chest-up, window light | First line: "Four years. Same spreadsheet." | Text: "4 years running close at a 9-person firm" | Payoff at: 0:45 | Why: the founder's history is the strongest proof available; weaker on a muted feed because it needs the face to land.
3. Pattern: side by side | Visual: split frame, spreadsheet left, close board right, same 6 sample clients | First line: "Same clients. Two views." | Text: "Spreadsheet vs close board" | Payoff at: 0:02 | Why: shows the shift instantly; risk is the right half is a prototype, so the label must be legible at phone size.

Recommended: 1, because the spreadsheet is the alternative the buyer lives in, and it works muted, which is how LinkedIn autoplays.
