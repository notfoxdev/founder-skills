# Launch video playbook

Read this in step 3 to pick a concept and in step 6 to time its beats. Six concepts cover nearly every startup launch. Pick by stage first (what exists to show), then audience (how they decide), then platform.

## Pick the length

| Length | Use for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30s | Feeds, ads, the vertical cut, feature launches to existing users | One flow only. Proof shrinks to one line or one text card |
| 60s | Default launch master: landing page, Product Hunt, LinkedIn, X | One job, 2 to 3 product actions |
| 90s | Considered B2B buyers, landing page `how-it-works`, YouTube | Only when 3 flows are needed to make the one job clear. Over 90s, use the chaptered demo in `formats.md` |

## Pick the concept by stage

| Stage | Strong | Possible | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Idea or validation (nothing filmable) | Problem-first with a labelled concept, founder story | Manifesto teaser for the waitlist | Watch it work, customer-led |
| MVP or beta (core flow works) | Watch it work, before/after | Problem-first | Customer-led until permission exists |
| Early revenue (paying customers) | Customer-led, before/after | Watch it work | Manifesto as the main video |
| Feature launch to existing users | Watch it work at 30s | Before/after | Founder story, manifesto |

## Pick the concept by audience

| Audience | Lean toward | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B2B, considered purchase | Problem-first, before/after; 60 to 90s master plus 1:1 for LinkedIn | The buyer must recognise their workflow and read the UI |
| Prosumer or consumer | Watch it work; short-form first | The decision is fast; the outcome sells |
| Developers | Watch it work at real speed in the terminal or IDE; music low or none | Polish and hidden speed-ups cost trust |
| Brand or community-led | Manifesto teaser, then watch it work | The belief earns the follow, the product earns the signup |

Decision rule: if two concepts tie, pick the one whose first frame works muted on the primary platform.

## Beat timings

All times in seconds from the start. The hook is always 0 to 2. The CTA always includes an end card held 2 to 3s with the URL. Scale proportionally for other lengths.

### 1. Problem-first

Opens inside the pain the viewer lives with, then turns to the product.

| Beat | 30s | 60s | 90s | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | The painful moment, mid-action, with the line the customer actually says |
| Problem | 2 to 7 | 2 to 14 | 2 to 20 | The current workaround shown, 2 to 3 quick shots, no acting |
| Turn | 7 to 9 | 14 to 16 | 20 to 23 | One short line or a hard cut; the messy thing becomes the product |
| Product moment | 9 to 23 | 16 to 44 | 23 to 68 | 2 to 3 actions (1 at 30s), same sample data as the problem beat |
| Proof | 23 to 26 | 44 to 52 | 68 to 80 | Real result, permissioned customer, or honest stage line |
| CTA | 26 to 30 | 52 to 60 | 80 to 90 | One action spoken and shown, end card |

Risk: a long problem beat. Keep it under 25% of runtime.

### 2. Before/after

The same task done the old way, then the new way, ideally on the same data.

| Beat | 30s | 60s | 90s | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | Split frame, both sides mid-task, or the "after" result first |
| Before | 2 to 8 | 2 to 12 | 2 to 18 | The old way at its real pace (a sped-up "before" is unfair, so do not) |
| Wipe | 8 to 10 | 12 to 14 | 18 to 21 | A wipe or match cut on the same object |
| After | 10 to 22 | 14 to 40 | 21 to 60 | Same task, same data, new way |
| Result | 22 to 26 | 40 to 50 | 60 to 75 | Final states side by side; compare time or steps only if measured |
| CTA | 26 to 30 | 50 to 60 | 75 to 90 | One action, end card |

Risk: comparing against a named competitor. Compare against the workaround, or keep competitor claims factual, current, and sourced.

### 3. Watch it work (demo-first)

Outcome first, then how. The product is the story.

| Beat | 30s | 60s | 90s | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | The finished result on screen, or the first action already in motion |
| Rewind | 2 to 4 | 2 to 5 | 2 to 6 | "Here's how": back to the starting state |
| Flow 1 | 4 to 14 | 5 to 22 | 6 to 30 | The core action at real speed, zoomed to the detail |
| Flow 2 | 14 to 22 | 22 to 38 | 30 to 55 | The second action or the detail that makes it work |
| Flow 3 | none | 38 to 46 | 55 to 70 | An edge case buyers worry about, handled on screen |
| Proof | 22 to 26 | 46 to 53 | 70 to 80 | Real usage, permissioned customer, or stage line |
| CTA | 26 to 30 | 53 to 60 | 80 to 90 | One action, end card |

Requires a working product. Speed-ups are disclosed on screen ("2x") whenever speed is part of the claim.

### 4. Founder story

A true moment from the founder's own work, reaching the product by 10s.

| Beat | 30s | 60s | 90s | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | Founder to camera, mid-sentence, on the specific moment |
| Origin | 2 to 8 | 2 to 9 | 2 to 10 | The job, the years, the worst part; one concrete detail |
| Turn | 8 to 10 | 9 to 11 | 10 to 13 | "So we built..." with a cut to the product (labelled if concept) |
| Product moment | 10 to 22 | 11 to 42 | 13 to 65 | Screen or motion; founder VO continues |
| Why now / proof | 22 to 25 | 42 to 50 | 65 to 78 | Stage line, pilots, credential; all from `company.md` |
| CTA | 25 to 30 | 50 to 60 | 78 to 90 | Founder back on camera or VO, end card |

Risk: a preamble. If the product is not on screen by 10 to 13s, cut the origin.

### 5. Manifesto / teaser

A belief line over the customer's world. A teaser format, 15 to 60s.

| Beat | 15s | 30s | 60s | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | The belief line as text over a real object from the customer's world |
| World | 2 to 6 | 2 to 10 | 2 to 25 | 3 to 6 quick shots of the customer's reality, real environments |
| The line | 6 to 9 | 10 to 15 | 25 to 35 | What you believe, said plainly, one sentence |
| Glimpse | 9 to 12 | 15 to 22 | 35 to 48 | One product frame (labelled if concept) or none |
| Date / CTA | 12 to 15 | 22 to 30 | 48 to 60 | Launch date or waitlist, end card |

Not for 90s, and not the main video for a B2B buyer who needs to see how it works. Pair it with a problem-first or watch-it-work master.

### 6. Customer-led

A real customer, their workflow, their result. Needs written permission to appear and to state the result.

| Beat | 30s | 60s | 90s | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | 0 to 2 | The customer's result line in their own words, or their workplace mid-task |
| Before | 2 to 8 | 2 to 12 | 2 to 18 | Their old way, in their words |
| Product in use | 8 to 20 | 12 to 40 | 18 to 62 | Screen in their workflow, with sample or permitted data |
| Result | 20 to 25 | 40 to 50 | 62 to 78 | Their real number, approved by them |
| CTA | 25 to 30 | 50 to 60 | 78 to 90 | One action, end card |

No permission yet: write the script with `[NEED: customer consent + approved result]` and ship a different concept now.

## Checks before locking the concept

- The first frame works muted and at phone size.
- The same sample data runs through every scene, so the viewer follows one story.
- Every proof line traces to `company.md`, the founder, or a permission on file.
- The product is on screen by 30% of runtime in every concept except the manifesto teaser (watch it work: by 10%).
