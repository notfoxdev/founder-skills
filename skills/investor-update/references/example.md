**Request:** "Write this month's update for our 4 angels."

## Notes to the founder (not part of the email)

- **Lowlights are real, not filler.** You may want to lead with the pilots. The angels will read "2 pilots" as progress either way; what they cannot see without you telling them is that the pilots are free and that 2 pilots cannot reach your 3-firm goal. Saying it now makes November's result credible.
- **Labels.** The pilots are "pilots", not "customers" or "launched". The waitlist is "signups", not "users".
- **Standing metric set.** These five metrics become the set for every update. When the first firm pays, MRR and firms paying start moving and interviews may be dropped; if so, say so in that update with the reason.
- **Asks.** Both are criteria, not names. If you know which angel is closest to bookkeeping firms, name them in ask 1.
