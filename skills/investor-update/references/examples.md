# Investor update examples

> **ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY.** ExampleCo, its people, customers, investors, and every number below are fictional placeholders invented to show structure and tone. Do not copy any figure, name, or claim into a real update. Real updates use only founder-provided data.

ExampleCo (fictional) sells scheduling software to independent physiotherapy clinics. Seed stage, B2B SaaS.

---

## 1. Regular monthly update

**Subject:** ExampleCo: March 2099 update

Hi all,

**TL;DR**
- MRR grew 6% to $41.2k, below our 10% plan.
- Our largest customer (8% of MRR) gave 60-day notice of churn.
- Ask: intros to heads of operations at multi-site clinic groups (criteria below).

**Key metrics**

| Metric | Mar | Feb | Change | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MRR | $41.2k | $38.9k | +$2.3k (+6%) | Plan was +10% |
| Paying clinics | 118 | 109 | +9 (+8%) | |
| Logo churn (monthly) | 1.8% | 0.9% | +0.9 pts | 2 clinics closed |
| Trial-to-paid | 22% | 24% | -2 pts | |
| Net burn | $61k | $58k | +$3k (+5%) | First support hire |
| Runway | 14 mo | 15 mo | -1 mo | |

**Highlights**
- Shipped two-way calendar sync; 41% of clinics enabled it in the first two weeks.
- Signed our first 6-site group on an annual contract ($9.6k ACV).

**Lowlights**
- Our largest customer is leaving at the end of May. They are consolidating onto their EHR vendor's built-in scheduler. This takes about $3.3k off MRR.
- Missed the MRR plan by 4 points. Outbound to single-site clinics produced 3 deals against a target of 8.

**What we learned**
- Single-site clinics buy through peers, not outbound. We are stopping cold outbound to this segment and moving that time to a referral offer.
- EHR bundling is a real threat for groups. We are prioritising the integration that makes us the scheduler inside their EHR rather than beside it.

**Priorities for April**
1. Launch referral offer to existing clinics; target 10 referred trials.
2. Ship EHR integration beta with 3 design-partner groups.
3. Hire a second account manager for groups.

**Asks**
1. Intros to heads of operations at clinic groups with 5+ sites in the UK or Ireland, so we can test selling to groups. This month.
2. A 30-minute call with anyone who has competed against a feature an incumbent bundles for free.

Thanks to Jordan (fictional) for last month's intro to two clinic owners: one is now trialling.

**Cash & runway**
- Cash: $856k | Net burn (3-mo avg): $60k/mo | Runway: 14 months

Thanks,
Sam (fictional)

## 3. Crisis / pivot update

**Subject:** ExampleCo: important update on runway and plan

Hi all,

We are stopping sales to single-site clinics and focusing only on multi-site groups. This cuts our expected MRR growth for the next two quarters, and we are reducing burn to extend runway from 7 to 13 months.

**What happened**
- Single-site churn reached 4.1% monthly in Q1 (from 1.2% in Q3). Exit interviews with 14 churned clinics: 11 cited price, 9 closed or merged.
- Groups (5+ sites) are 31% of MRR, have 0.4% monthly churn, and 3x the ACV.

**What we have decided**
- Single-site: no new sales; existing customers stay supported.
- Team: reduced from 9 to 6. Two roles in single-site sales and one in marketing. All three received [severance terms: placeholder].
- Net burn falls from $78k to $42k per month from June.

**Cash & runway**
- Cash: $546k | Net burn from June: $42k/mo | Runway: ~13 months (was 7)

**What is still open**
- Whether the group motion reaches $60k MRR within 9 months. If not, we will tell you by month 6 and lay out options.

**What we need**
1. Intros to operations leaders at clinic groups with 5+ sites. This is now the only thing that moves the company.
2. A 30-minute call in the next two weeks with anyone who has cut a company down to one segment.

I called our lead investor before sending this. Happy to talk with anyone individually this week.

Sam (fictional)

## Notes on what these examples do

- The TL;DR names the bad news (churn notice, missed plan) instead of hiding it.
- Metric changes are calculated, not described ("+6%", not "strong growth").
- Lowlights state the miss first, then the reason in one clause.
- Asks name criteria and timing. With real targets, the founder would name the person and company. The regular update thanks last month's helper by name and says what came of it.
- The voice is the founder's: "we", plain verbs, the miss stated as a miss ("Outbound to single-site clinics produced 3 deals against a target of 8"), not "headwinds".
- The warm-up omits cash and runway and makes a non-money ask.
- The crisis update puts the decision in the first sentence and includes runway before and after.
