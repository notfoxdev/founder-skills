# Raise plan: Ledgerline

> General information, not legal or financial advice. Confirm instruments and terms with counsel.

## Readiness
**Verdict:** raise after the pilots convert (~6 weeks). Prepare now so the process starts the week the conversion decisions land.

**Why:**
- The first question every investor will ask is your biggest risk: will firms pay on top of Karbon or TaxDome? Today the answer is "we don't know". The pilots answer it by about 2026-11-25. Raising now means pitching the open question five weeks before you could pitch the answer.
- 11 interviews and founder-market fit clear the pre-seed bar at the margin. 2 free pilots are a time commitment, not a money commitment. A paid conversion moves you from "interesting" to "evidence".
- The 62 waitlist signups will not carry a raise: 9 ÷ 62 = 15% are in your 3–15 staff band.

**Runway at close:** 7 − 1.5 (pilots end, conversion decision by ~2026-12-04) − 2 (active process) = 3.5 months. That clears the 3-month floor, but only if burn stays flat and the process does not slip. Late December is usually slow for investor meetings, so the A-tier window realistically opens in January.

**Trigger date: 2026-12-04.**
| Pilot result by 2026-12-04 | Decision |
|---|---|
| 2 of 2 paying ≥ $150/month | Start intros the week of 2026-12-07 |
| 1 of 2 paying | Start anyway with that evidence and the third pilot's status; cut burn in parallel |
| 0 paying | Do not raise on this story. Run idea-validator and pricing-strategy on why, and cut burn to buy time |

**Milestones that change the odds:**
1. Pilot firms paying at least $150/month after day 30 (answers the biggest risk directly).
2. A third firm paying, which is your 90-day goal. Pushback: 2 pilots cannot reach 3 paying firms. Offer a third pilot to the 9 in-band waitlist firms this week.
3. [NEED: pilot firms still using it in their second close cycle], a retention signal investors will ask about.
