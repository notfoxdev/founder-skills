# Investor questions

Pick the 15 most likely for this company. Weight by stage, business model, and `## Biggest risk` in company.md: the biggest risk generates at least 3 questions. Answers use only founder-provided data or the journal. Missing data: write `[NEED: ...]` and an honest answer with a plan.

## Answer rules
- Lead with the answer in one sentence, then one piece of evidence, then stop.
- Numbers: value, period, definition. "MRR $X in <month>, up Y% from <month>."
- "I don't know yet" is acceptable when followed by how and when you will know.
- Never guess a number in a meeting. Offer to follow up.
- Do not attack competitors; state the wedge.

## Question bank

**Team**
1. Why are you the right team for this?
2. How did you meet, and how do you split decisions?
3. Who is full-time, and what is each founder's equity?
4. What key hire is missing, and when do you make it?

**Problem and customer**
5. Who exactly is the customer, and who signs the cheque?
6. How do you know this is a hair-on-fire problem, not a nice-to-have?
7. What do customers do today instead?
8. How often does the problem occur, and what does it cost them?

**Product and insight**
9. What do you know that others don't?
10. Why now?
11. What is hard to copy here in two years?
12. Show me how it works.

**Traction and evidence**
13. What are your key metrics, and how have they moved month over month?
14. What is retention (or repeat usage) by cohort?
15. How did you get your first 10 customers, and does that scale?
16. What is the biggest thing you've learned from customers that changed the product?

**Market**
17. How big can this get? Walk me through the math.
18. What is the expansion path after the initial wedge?

**Business model and go-to-market**
19. How do you price, and have you tested it?
20. What is the sales cycle (B2B) / acquisition cost (B2C)?
21. Which channel works today, and which is next?
22. What are gross margins now and at scale?

**Competition**
23. Who else is doing this, and why will you win?
24. What happens if <incumbent> builds this feature?

**Round**
25. How much are you raising, and on what instrument?
26. What milestone does this money reach, and when?
27. Who else is in the round / who is leading?
28. What does your runway look like today?

**Risk**
29. What kills this company?
30. What would make you quit or pivot?

## Tough-question patterns
- **"What if X copies you?"** State the wedge and what compounds (data, distribution, workflow lock-in) with evidence, or admit the gap and the plan.
- **"Why hasn't this worked before?"** Name the prior attempt if known (founder to verify) and what changed (why now).
- **"Your growth is slowing."** Acknowledge with the number, give the cause, say what you changed.
- **"Valuation?"** Give the round size and milestone first. Defer terms to the lead conversation; confirm terms with counsel.
