# Worked example: plan-review

> Illustrative and fictional (Ledgerline fixture). Calibrates depth and format only; never reuse its content.

## Request

"Review my plan: launch on Product Hunt in two weeks, start a newsletter, and hire a part-time SDR."

## Context used

- `.foundora/company.md`: Ledgerline, 2 free pilots, no paying customers, 7 months of runway, solo founder. 90-day goal: 5 paying firms.
- Mode default: Reduce, because the plan has three tracks and the evidence supports none of them yet.

## Model answer (abridged)

# Plan review: November growth push (2026-10-23)

**Verdict:** rethink. Your goal is 5 paying firms and none of the three tracks gets you a paying firm sooner than asking the two pilots to convert.
**Mode:** Reduce (you can say Hold instead)

## Decisions by door
| Decision | Door | Size | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product Hunt launch | two-way | small | defer; no paying customer to show yet |
| Newsletter | two-way | small | cut for now |
| Part-time SDR | one-way | big | slow down: 3 months of salary is 40% of runway |

## Top issues
1. Nobody pays yet: the SDR would sell a price that hasn't been tested → convert the two pilots at $150 first.
2. Three tracks for one person: each would get a third of your week → pick one.
3. Product Hunt brings accountants who aren't your buyer → launch when there's a case study.

## Deferred
- SDR — why: no tested price or pitch — revisit when: 3 firms pay and you can't keep up with demos.
